02 Protocol

Six layers of guarantees.
Every one enforced by code.

Each failure has a structural fix, and every fix is a Pollar primitive. Remove any one of the six, and the failure it closes comes back.

01

One-tap onboarding

Residents sign in with Google or email. Pollar provisions a Stellar wallet in seconds — no seed phrase, no extension, no install.

02

KYC before funds

Deferred funding means a wallet isn't activated until identity clears. Anonymous participation in a treasury is impossible.

03

Two signatures, always

Every vendor payment requires two officers to sign in the app. No single human — including the estate chairman — can move funds alone.

04

Yield on idle balance

Treasury funds sit in a DeFindex vault earning live APY between contribution day and vendor payment day. Yield accrues to the estate.

05

x402 auto-pay agent

Each resident approves a monthly USD limit once. An x402 agent signs the Soroban authorization and pays automatically. Pollar's facilitator covers gas.

06

Immutable ledger

Every contribution, every disbursement, every yield accrual — written to Postgres and to the Stellar ledger. Queryable by every resident, every time.
